2.1 Kinetic Particle Model of Matter
1
Solids: High density, fixed volume and rigid shape. Particles vibrate about fixed points inside regular lattice formations.
Liquids: Medium-high density, constant volumetric configuration but conforms to container geometries. Particles change neighborhoods dynamically.
Gases: Extremely low density, occupies any configuration volume. High velocity independent molecular paths.
4 Brownian Motion: Random micro-particle movements caused by consistent collisions with much smaller, fast-moving fluid molecules.
3.1 General Properties of Waves
1 Waves transfer energy without transferring mass. Standard wave relation formulation: $v = f\lambda$.
3
Longitudinal Waves: Structural oscillations are parallel to energy vectors.
Transverse Waves: Structural oscillations are perpendicular to energy vectors.
4.2 Electrical Quantities

5.2 Radioactivity
3

Gamma ($\gamma$): Electromagnetic radiation paths. High penetration potential, attenuated strictly by lead arrays.
